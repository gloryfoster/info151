Professional Associations

Though I am not currently a member of any professional organizations, there are a number I have my eyes on for the future, as I advance in my career!

Information Systems Security Information

The ISSA is a cybersecurity and information systems-based organization that is dedicated to further the careers of cybersecurity professionals. This is done through training, networking, and special community events.

Women in Cybersecurity

WiCyS is a cybersecurity professional organization, specifically dedicated to the furthering of a diverse and inclusive computer science workforce. The organization focuses on ways to get more women involved in the cybersecurity space, while also providing resources and networking opportunities for those already a part of it.

National Board of Medical Examiners

An education-centered medical organization, dedicated to teaching and certifying medical professionals. Additionally spends time researching methods and structures for improving education. I am to join not only to assist in these research projects, but also to work on website and database design for the organization.

International Game Developers Association

An organization dedicated to creating community and networking opportunities for game designs and developers, as well as those wishing to enter the space. The group offers gatherings and events for professional development, connecting and networking with others in the field, and hearing from experts.
